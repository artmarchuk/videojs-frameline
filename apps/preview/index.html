    Try this

    Six things a creator does with the Studio monitor. Click the player first: its shortcuts work while it has focus. Each button does the task for you.

    1. Step to the exact frame

      Press , and . to move one frame back or forward. Hold Shift with ← or → to jump ten.

    2. Set a range and loop it

      Press I and O at the playhead, or drag the handles at the ends of the timeline. Play: the range loops. R switches Loop off, and playback then stops at Out.

    3. Check a vertical cut

      Open Guides and choose TikTok under Platform zones. It masks the picture to 9:16 and marks where the app puts its own interface, roughly.

    4. Check title safe

      Open Guides and choose Title safe. Text inside the frame stays clear of the edges on any screen.

    5. Zoom to 200% and pan

      Press Z to step through the zoom levels, or use the Zoom button. Drag the picture, or press Shift Alt and an arrow, to move around.

    6. Grab a frame

      Press G to save the frame on screen as a PNG, at the video's own size and without the guides.

    About

    Frameline is a Video.js skin for the tools people use to make video: editors, generators and review platforms. Viewer is the clean player a creator's client or audience gets, and Studio is the creator's own monitor, with frame-by-frame transport, timecode, a range to loop, guides, zoom and frame grabs. It adds controls and overlays around the picture and never changes the picture itself.

    Dark by design: a neutral dark surround keeps judgements of exposure and contrast honest.
