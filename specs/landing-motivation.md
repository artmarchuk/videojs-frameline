# Landing page: the motivation paragraph

The "Frameline Brand Content" doc in the claude.ai project has a new section, "Motivation": one paragraph on why Frameline exists. Its placements table lists it for the landing intro (status "To add"). This change adds it to the page. Page code only: nothing under `src/` changes, no screenshots are re-shot.

Work on a new branch, `landing-motivation`, from `main`. Deploy a preview; the author merges.

## The copy

Exactly this, character for character (from the doc):

> A lot of video is now made in the browser: cut in web editors, generated by AI video tools, approved on review platforms. But the player inside these tools is usually built for watching. It has play, seek and volume, and often nothing to check the work with: no timecode to quote, no way to step a single frame, no range to loop, no safe area to place a title against. So people check their video somewhere else, or they guess. Frameline adds an editor's preview monitor to the Video.js player for the people making the video, and keeps a clean Viewer for the people watching it.

Don't edit it. "often nothing" and "A lot of video" are deliberate (see the doc).

## Design

- **Section frame:** the same structure as the other sections: `<section id="why" class="wrap section" aria-labelledby="why-title">`, the ruler with the label "Why", then the section body.
- **Heading:** an `h2` with the text "Why Frameline exists", visually hidden (the existing `.visually-hidden` class). The ruler label is the visible marker; heading order stays h1, h2, h3.
- **The paragraph:** one `<p>`, left-aligned with the other section headings, `max-width: 680px`, in `--type-lead` (17 px; 16 px on phones through the Phone mode). Line height as the lead style.
- **Two tones in one paragraph:** the problem (everything up to "or they guess.") in `--dim`; the last sentence, the answer ("Frameline adds … watching it."), in `--text`, wrapped in a `<span>`. No bold, no italics, no pull-quote marks, no icons. The tone shift does the work.
- **Spacing:** the section's own tokens (`--layout-section-top`, `--layout-section-bottom`), as the other ruled sections. No new tokens.
- Nothing animates.
